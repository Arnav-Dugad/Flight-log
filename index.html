Your atlas belongs to you.

Flight Log stores account details, preferences and journeys in Firebase under your authenticated user ID. The interface contains no demo journeys and does not publish a social profile.

  • User-isolated recordsFirestore rules in this repository restrict each user path to that signed-in user.
  • No public timelineYour routes remain inside your account unless you export or share a Flightprint.
  • Portable by defaultExport your complete journey collection as JSON at any time.
